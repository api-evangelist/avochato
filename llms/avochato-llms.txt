# AVOCHATO LLM GUIDE

> Avochato leverages the most advanced Large Language Models (LLMs) to automate, scale, and enhance business communications across SMS, RCS, MMS, Calls, and Live Chat.

## Models Supported:
- o3, GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1-mini, GPT-4.1-nano, GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini (OpenAI)
- Claude sonnet-4, Claude 3.7-sonnet, Claude 3.5-haiku, Claude 3.5-sonnet (Anthropic) 

## AI and Automation Capabilities:
- Automated responses from AI agents to prospects, customers, and staff
- Multi-language support providing automated responses in over 50 languages  
- Automatic escalation to human teammates based on tags and message content
- Fully configurable agent instructions, prompting settings, response logic, and auto-assignment
- AI-generated suggested messages for incoming messages
- Conversation thread summarization and action item identification
- Sentiment analysis to prioritize urgent conversations
- Lead qualification, tagging, and scoring
- Dynamic routing to the right agent or department
- Tone adjustment and grammar recommendations for brand consistency
- Image detection and tagging for MMS and attachments 
- Automatic redaction of images containing PII or other sensitive content

- Recruiting: ask pre-qualifying questions over text, schedule and confirm interviews, send reminders to reduce no-shows

- Scheduling: let prospects self-schedule appointments via SMS, automate reminders, and share calendar links
- Customer support: auto-route incoming chats, handle multiple threads simultaneously, and track team performance in real time

## Channel Support:
- SMS: ubiquitous messaging with proven deliverability
- MMS: share and receive images, GIFs, videos, and emojis to enrich conversations
- RCS: branded business profiles, interactive messaging with read receipts, carousels, and typing indicators
- Calls: make and receive calls via shared inbox, call forwarding, in-browser and mobile-app calls; programmable-voice bots and call trees
- Live Chat: embeddable web widget, seamless hand-off to SMS, unified inbox with AI agents for 24/7 support

## Advanced Analytics and Power Features:
- Sentiment analysis: score conversations on a scale of 1 to 10, compare bot and agent satisfaction, and automate daily or weekly sentiment tracking
- Real-time dashboards: reports on SMS, MMS, calls, and chat activity with downloadable CSV exports
- Broadcasting: send a personalized text to a large audience instantly, with segmentation options such as tags and custom fields
- Templates: auto-reply to common queries, link directly to broadcasts, campaigns, and surveys
- Campaigns: schedule multi-step, dynamic-field cadences with unlimited follow-ups and end conditions
- Tags and keywords: auto-tag contacts on keyword triggers; segment audiences; API webhooks for dynamic tagging
